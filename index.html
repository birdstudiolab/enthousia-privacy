Enthousia

Privacy policy

Version 1.0 · effective 30 September 2026 · applies to Enthousia for iPhone. The seller name shown on the App Store product page is the contracting party for this purchase.

The short version. Enthousia has no server. What you type and what it writes back are stored in the app's own storage inside your iPhone and never leave it. The app contains no networking code at all, so this is not a promise about how we might treat your data — there is no route for it to travel, and no one on our side could read it if we wanted to.

What a reading is, so the rest of this page makes sense

You write one question. Enthousia selects three figures from a book of eighteen that ships inside the app, and assembles a ten-section document about them and your question. The selection and the assembly are arithmetic over text that was written by hand and packaged with the app. There is no language model in the product, no prompt leaving the phone, and no answer being generated by a service somewhere. Ask the same question and draw the same three figures and you get the same document back — which is a property of how it is assembled, not of something deciding in the moment.

What we do not collect

  • No account, no sign-up, no email address, nothing to remember a password for.
  • No analytics and no usage telemetry. There is no third-party SDK in the app to send anything.
  • No advertising, and therefore no advertising identifier.
  • No crash reporting service. If something breaks, the app says so on screen instead of phoning anyone.
  • No location, no contacts, no health data, no photos.
  • No question and no reading is uploaded anywhere. There is no endpoint to upload to.

The app asks for no permissions

Enthousia never shows a permission dialog. It has no camera access, no microphone, no access to your photo library in either direction, no access to your contacts or calendar. The only thing it needs from outside itself is the App Store transaction that unlocks the full reading, and that conversation is between your device and Apple.

What is stored on your device

  • Your questions, exactly as you typed them.
  • The readings they produced: the date, the theme, the three figures, and the full text of each section.
  • Which figure is on today's page, which is derived from the calendar rather than recorded about you.
  • Whether the full reading is unlocked on this Apple Account, and whether the app has already asked you to rate it.

The readings are written to a single file in the app's own storage, protected with iOS's highest data-protection class, which means it is encrypted while the phone is locked. It is not backed up to any Enthousia service, because there is none. iCloud backup behaviour is governed by Apple's normal rules for app documents, not by us.

Purchases

The full reading — all ten sections, roughly 1,400 words, for every question you ask — is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. Asking a question, the free opening section, today's figure and your archive stay free and need no purchase. We receive only the transaction information that StoreKit exposes to the app on your device, which is what lets Restore purchase work when you reinstall on a new phone. We never see your name, address, card details or receipt email, and nothing renews: there is no subscription in this product.

Your data, your hands

Swiping a reading in the Archive reveals Delete, which removes that question and its text from the device at once. Deleting the app removes everything else it holds. There is no request to file with us and no waiting period, because there is nothing on our side to remove.

If you stop using the app, your readings stay in your iPhone's storage until you delete them or delete the app. There is no export, because there is nothing to sync with.

About the content

Enthousia writes about figures from Greek myth and the people who have read them for two and a half thousand years. It is a mirror, not a diagnosis. It is not medical, psychological, financial or legal advice, and it does not predict the future. Nothing in it was produced by looking at you: the text was written before you downloaded the app, and it is the same text everyone with the same three figures gets. We do not ask you to tell us anything about your health, and the app is not a place to make a decision about it.

Children

Enthousia is directed at adults who read myth. It is not a children's product, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the version and date at the top of this page change, and the same page is updated. Continued use after an update means you accept the updated terms; in practice, a policy that promises "we hold nothing, and the code cannot reach the network" has very little room to grow.
